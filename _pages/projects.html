Explore the programs and initiatives through which we turn our mission into action.

Scholarship Grants

Empower underprivileged students by providing sustained financial assistance, learning resources, and pathways to a brighter academic future.

Educational Outreaches

Spark curiosity, creativity, and foundational skills in young learners through engaging, full-day school workshops.

Our Educational Outreaches come in four flavors:

Academics Daycamp

Introduces students to fun physics, chemistry, mathematics, and robotics concepts through hands-on experiments and informative exhibits.

Creative Arts & Values Daycamp

Aims to stimulate creativity through various painting and crafting activities, while reinforcing concepts of morals, values, and camaraderie tailored for young kids.

Foundational Literacy Workshop

Engages students through reading, storytelling, and improv activities in order to boost their confidence and learning ability.

Astrocamp & Space Discovery

Aims to enrich students' appreciation of nature, ourselves, and the world around us by giving them a window into the cosmos.

Feeding Programs

Promote healthy development by serving balanced, nutrient-dense meals alongside nutrition education during key seasons.

Medical Missions

Safeguard and improve children's health by offering free check-ups and delivering vital prescription medicines and dietary supplements directly to local communities.

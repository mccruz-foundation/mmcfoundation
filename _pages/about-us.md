M&M Cruz BEACON Foundation, Inc. is formed to establish, operate, and maintain a non-stock, non-profit foundation dedicated to improving the lives of disadvantaged, abandoned, neglected, and orphaned children through education, shelter, healthcare, and community development programs, and to do all acts necessary or incidental to the achievement of this purpose. 

## Vision statement

We envision communities where every abandoned, neglected, and orphaned child is met with *dignity*, *opportunity*, and the quiet, faithful *care* that changes a life.
Ultimately, we idealize a Philippines where no child is left to circumstance—where every child, however they begin, grows up safe, healthy, educated, and valued.

## Mission statement

We walk alongside children the world has overlooked—funding their schooling, sheltering them in safety, tending to their health, and building up the communities around them.

Become a Member

Looking to get more involved with the Foundation and make a longer-term commitment? Become a Regular Member!

Regular Members are more deeply involved in the Foundation and may have opportunities to participate in committees, organizational planning, and governance. Membership is a great fit if you want to be part of the Foundation beyond individual projects and help shape what we do.

If you're interested in becoming a Regular Member, fill out the form below and tell us a little about yourself and how you'd like to get involved. If you have any questions, please contact support@mmcruzfoundation.org.

Ready to join us? Sign up below!

Become a General Volunteer

Don't have a lot of time to commit, or prefer to get involved on a project-by-project basis? General Volunteering might be a great fit for you!

General Volunteers help out with specific projects, events, and activities based on their interests, skills, and availability. When you sign up, let us know what kinds of projects you'd be interested in, what skills or experience you can contribute, and how you'd like to help.

We'll reach out when an opportunity comes up that matches what you're looking for.

No long-term commitment required — just help out when you can, with projects you care about! If you have any questions, please contact support@mmcruzfoundation.org.
